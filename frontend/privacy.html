1. Introduction & Architectural Privacy

Lemma AI is designed with a strict academic privacy focus. We recognize that research drafts, theses, unpublished discoveries, and proprietary datasets require rigorous protection. This policy explains what information is collected, how document processing is executed, and how we interact with third-party academic search APIs.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Full name, institutional or personal email address, encrypted password hash (via bcrypt), and academic role (student, researcher, or institutional admin).
  • Research Inputs: Research topics, domain specifications, paper formatting preferences (e.g. IEEE, Springer, ACM, APA), and user-submitted text prompts.
  • Uploaded Documents: PDF, DOCX, and text files submitted for manuscript restructuring or similarity checking.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized browser information, session timestamps, and device viewport specifications necessary for responsive interface rendering.

3. Verified Processing Pipeline & Technologies

In accordance with our commitment to transparency, the following technologies and processing methods are verified as actively implemented in Lemma AI:

  • Text & Document Extraction: Files submitted for restructuring are parsed using pypdf, python-docx, and Optical Character Recognition (OCR via pytesseract/pdf2image) solely to extract textual content for layout conversion.
  • Local NLP & Embeddings: Text segmentation and semantic matching are conducted locally using spaCy and Sentence-Transformers (MiniLM bi-encoder models).
  • Database & Vector Indexing: User account data, generated papers, and document reference indices are maintained in a PostgreSQL database (optionally utilizing the pgvector extension or embedded in-memory BM25 index). No external cloud file storage buckets (e.g. AWS S3 or Google Cloud Storage) are currently used.
  • External Academic Query APIs: To verify citations and discover scholarly prior art, high-level non-confidential search terms derived from topics are queried against publicly accessible academic repositories: the arXiv API, Crossref Metadata API, Semantic Scholar API, and Wikipedia API. Full confidential manuscripts are never transmitted to these public index APIs.
  • Inference Providers: Paper synthesis is executed via configured language model endpoints, which may include Groq Cloud (Llama 3.3), OpenAI API (when server keys are configured), or a local Ollama instance.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Lemma AI utilizes browser localStorage for strictly essential functionality:

  • lemma_token / lemma_auth: Stores your JSON Web Token (JWT) session to keep you securely signed in.
  • lemma-theme / lemma-sidebar-state: Remembers your dark/light display preference and navigation collapse state.
  • lemma_cookie_consent: Stores your explicit cookie preference.

Optional analytics telemetry is disabled by default and is only initialized if a valid measurement ID is configured and you grant explicit cookie consent.

5. Data Retention & User Control

Users retain complete ownership over their research manuscripts. You may request the export or deletion of your account profile and stored manuscripts at any time by contacting our governance team at support@lemma.ai.

6. AI Output & Academic Integrity Disclaimer

Lemma AI provides automated assistance for drafting, structuring, and literature cross-referencing. AI-generated formulations and bibliographic citations must be independently reviewed and verified by the author. Lemma AI does not guarantee academic acceptance or error-free outputs, and authors remain personally responsible for complying with the academic integrity guidelines of their educational institution or publisher.
